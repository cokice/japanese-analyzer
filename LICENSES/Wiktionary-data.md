The pinned source snapshot keeps only records with `lang_code=ja` from the Korean edition. Dates and SHA-256 hashes of both the downloaded raw archive and the committed Japanese-language slice are in `dictionary.locales.lock.json`. The slice retains all extracted fields for those records. Generated application shards select definitions and textual examples, keep source URLs, deduplicate identical records and exclude ambiguous JMdict associations. They do not represent the full Korean Wiktionary, and are not aligned to English sense numbers.
